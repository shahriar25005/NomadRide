🎮 Game Controls

💻 Desktop Controls

  • • Gallop / Sprint: Hold SHIFT (burns extra Fuel)

  • • Mount / Dismount: Tap the compact yellow 🐎 Mount button on the bottom-right
  • • Gallop Boost: Hold the green ⚡ Gallop button on the bottom-right

📜 CrazyGames Guidelines

🍎 1. Fruit = Fuel & XP Survival Loop

Riding and galloping burns your orange Fuel bar. Eat wild Red Apples (+18 Fuel), Golden Sunberries (+36 Fuel & Speed Boost), and Blue Oasis Melons (100% Fuel + 110 XP) so your mount never stalls!

📍 2. Outpost Map-Pin Claiming

Stand inside any of the 25 black Map-Pin rings for 3 seconds to claim it for +250 XP, a fruit burst, and passive score every second you hold it.

🤝 3. CrazyGames Community & Fair Play

Keep nicknames friendly and respectful. Optional Rewarded Ads grant a temporary Golden Caravan Strider mount with full fuel and pause game audio cleanly during playback.
